Customer Policies

Last updated: September 2026

Privacy

We use customer account information, contact details, delivery address and order history to process orders, support customers, provide invoices and manage returns/refunds. Online payment credentials are handled by the payment gateway and are not stored by Madhu Enterprises.

Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation from their account while an order is in an eligible pre-dispatch status. Once an order has shipped or been delivered, cancellation may no longer be available and the customer can use the return process where applicable.

Returns & Refunds

Return eligibility depends on the product and its condition. Customers can submit a return request from My Orders after delivery. Approved online-payment refunds are processed back through the original payment method where supported by the payment gateway.

Payment

We support Cash on Delivery and online payment. Online payments are processed through the configured payment gateway. A payment can be shown as pending, paid, failed, refund pending or refunded in the order account.
